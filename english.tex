\cvsection{Academic Summary}

I'm currently a Computer Engineering student with a passion for astronomy and data science. I have experience in scientific research, with a focus on computational astrophysics, and a history of publications in national conferences. I'm always seeking to learn and contribute to the scientific community, exploring new technologies and methodologies to advance our understanding of the universe. I have a hobby in animations and simulations, which allows me to create engaging visualizations to communicate complex concepts in an accessible way, with a focus on science outreach of physics-related content.
