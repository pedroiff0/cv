I'm currently a Computer Engineering student with a passion for astronomy and data science. I have experience in scientific research, with a focus on computational astrophysics, and a history of publications in national conferences. I'm always seeking to learn and contribute to the scientific community, exploring new technologies and methodologies to advance our understanding of the universe.
